A file manager built like a workspace rather than a browser. Multiple folder tabs,
a preview pane, and an operation journal that survives the app being killed
mid-copy.

It opens straight into your storage. No picker to get through first, no setup, no
account.

THE WORKSPACE
• Multiple folder tabs, breadcrumbs, filtering and refresh.
• List, grid and details views, each adapting to the window you are in.
• Docked or floating preview panes.
• Layouts for phone, landscape, tablets, foldables and desktop-style Android.
• System, light and dark themes, accent presets and optional dynamic colour.

FILES, AND GETTING THEM BACK
• Create, rename, duplicate, copy and move.
• A recycle bin that never expires on its own. Nothing is deleted on a timer.
  Permanent deletion is always something you chose.
• A durable operation journal with progress, cancellation, and recovery after
  process death. If a copy is interrupted, you can see exactly where it stopped.
• Conflict policies for skip, keep both, and guarded replace.
• When a move copies successfully but the storage provider refuses to remove the
  original, a dedicated cleanup finishes the job without copying anything again.

PREVIEW
• Text, source code and Markdown.
• The formats that come with agent work: JSON, YAML, TOML, prompts, instructions,
  logs, diffs and patches.
• Images, including SVG, GIF and animated WebP.

CLOUD AND REMOTE STORAGE
Connect cloud, USB and third-party providers through Android's own storage access
framework, with the permission grants persisted so you are not re-asked constantly.

QUIET BY DESIGN
• No ads, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking.
• Nothing about your files is uploaded, indexed remotely or scanned.
• Optional AI-assisted organisation is bring-your-own-key, proposal-only, and never
  moves a file without your explicit approval.

Fylz is open source and local-first.
