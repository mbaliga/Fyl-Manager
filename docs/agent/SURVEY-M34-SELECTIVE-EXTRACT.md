## 10. Risks for the design (facts + the constraint each imposes)

1. **`FylzV1App.kt` ratchet is at zero headroom** (2287/2287; `FylzAppShell` 112/112). Extract-specific lines already there: `:220`, `:233-246`, `:422-425`, `:604-610`, `:617`, `:1007-1020`, `:1593-1619` (~55 lines). M3.3 §2.8 plans to move `BrowserState` construction out for ~10 lines and to lower the bound (`DESIGN-M33:385-392`). Any M3.4 UI (four actions, a target sheet, per-entry conflicts) must be net-negative here or live elsewhere.
2. **Queue is COPY/MOVE-shaped end to end:** gate `TransferWorker.kt:44-46`; `inputData` has no archive/selection/password fields (`:140-163`); `enqueueTransfer` signature (`OperationRunner.kt:142-150`); `recover` filter (`:209`); `OperationRetryPolicy.retryableTypes` (`:39-42`); `FylzAppShell` retry `error("Unsupported retry type.")` (`:92`); worker builds its own `FileOperationService`/journal (`TransferWorker.kt:60`) and `OperationJournal` instances are not live-synced (`OperationJournal.kt:29-33`).
3. **WorkManager `Data.MAX_DATA_BYTES = 10240`** (verified in `work-runtime-2.11.2` `classes.jar` via `javap`; `Data` is persisted in WorkManager's DB). `KEY_SOURCES` is a `String[]` of Uris (`TransferWorker.kt:155`); archive-provider Uris are ~250 chars at depth 1 (`DESIGN-M33:118-120`) → roughly 35–40 selected entries exhaust the budget; a list of `Int` ordinals is far cheaper but still bounded; large selections need an out-of-band store (journal rows, a file). Passwords must never enter `Data` (M3.9: `CharArray`, wiped, never persisted — `MASTER_PLAN.md:396-398`); today `extractPassword` is a Compose `String` (`FylzV1App.kt:425`).
4. **Double/triple write today** (stage archive → cache tree → provider; `ArchiveService.kt:207, 244-254`), modelled by `ArchiveSpacePolicy.requirements` (`:22-29`). Routing bulk extraction through `FileOperationService.copy(entryUris)` over the M3.3 provider would go through the **entry cache** (`ArchiveEntryCache`: fill per entry, cap `min(maxFileBytes, 512 MiB)`, ≤ 2 concurrent fills, a full decompression pass per entry on `tar.*`/solid 7z — "copying N files out of a `tar.gz` costs about N/2 full decompressions") plus `streamCopy` plus verification re-reads; M3.3 explicitly defers "one-pass bulk copy-out" to M3.4 and keeps `extract(Selection)` for it (`DESIGN-M33:267-311, 531-533, 534-540`). Constraint: bulk extraction must bypass `openDocument`-materialisation and stream `extract()` output straight to destinations.
5. **Policy limits vs the 5 GB acceptance** — see §6: `max_archive_bytes` 2 GiB, `max_file_bytes` 1 GiB, `max_total_uncompressed_bytes` 4 GiB refuse a 5 GB 7z three ways (`policy.rs:48-58, 146-182`; `lib.rs:704-732`), and the same numbers live in `ArchiveExtractionPolicy.kt:13-21`, `ArchiveSource` (M3.2 §2.3) and `ArchiveEntryCache` (M3.3 §2.4). `MASTER_PLAN.md:406` cannot be met without changing limits or making them destination-aware.
6. **`ArchiveExtractionPolicy.kt` deletion is scheduled for M3.4** (`DESIGN-M32:34-38`; header comment `ArchiveExtractionPolicy.kt:1`). HEAD callers: `ArchiveService.kt:50` (limits ctor default), `:156` (`inspectZip`), `:221` (`extractZip`); `createZip` uses `extractionLimits.maxFileBytes/maxTotalUncompressedBytes/maxArchiveBytes` (`:93, :98, :122, :128`) but not `evaluate`; `ArchiveInspection.extractionDecision` (`:42`) is rendered by `ArchiveToolsOverlay` (`:312, :331-335`). After M3.2c (planned) `inspectZip` and `data.ArchiveInspection` are deleted, leaving `extractZip` and `createZip` (`DESIGN-M32:392-395`); `REVIEW_QUEUE.md:178-237` records the three Rust-vs-Kotlin rule deviations (unknown compressed size, link rule, lossy names) that become user-visible when extraction moves. Tests going with it: `ArchiveExtractionPolicyTest` (8), `ArchiveExtractionPolicyFuzzTest` (4).
7. **`ExtendedArchiveBrowserService` (commons-compress) has no callers** (grep over `app/src`), yet `commons-compress:1.28.0`, `org.tukaani:xz:1.12` and `zip4j:2.11.5` remain in `app/build.gradle.kts:104-106`; zip4j is used only by `ArchiveService.kt`. Removal is M3.10 (`MASTER_PLAN.md:400`; `SURVEY-M32:198`).
8. **7z CRC mismatches are swallowed** by `Reader::check` accepting `ARCHIVE_WARN` (`lib.rs:438`; `7zip.c:1142-1160`), and Rust never inspects `archive_error_string` after a WARN. "with verification" for 7z therefore has no engine-side signal today; ZIP mismatches are `Fatal` (`zip.c:3473-3486`).
9. **Progress semantics:** the worker's `totalBytes` is per file (`FileOperationService.kt:203, 245`), the notification is item-count only (`TransferWorker.kt:104`), and Rust reports bytes per entry only at `done` (`lib.rs:1005`). A 5 GB single-entry extract has no byte progress source unless the drain counts.
10. **`DecoderClient` at HEAD** is single-call, 5 s, no streaming (`DecoderClient.kt:47, 57, 111-131`); M3.2b/c are pending (`PROGRESS.md:87`) and M3.3's `callStreaming`/idle-unbind/generation guard are design-only. M3.4 cannot start its IPC until those land.
