# Working-name risk: Fylz

Checked on **2 August 2026**.

## Finding

The working name is not cleanly unoccupied:

- `fylz.in` currently uses **Fylz** for a browser-based phone-to-PC file-transfer service.
- `fylz.ai` currently uses **FYLZ** for an AI document-management platform that classifies, organizes, renames and routes files.

Both are close to this app's intended product category. That creates a practical risk of user confusion, store-listing friction and potential trademark conflict even though this repository is open source.

## Decision for the foundation

- Keep the repository name `Fyl-Manager` and package `io.github.mbaliga.fylz` for this technical prototype.
- Treat **Fylz** as a provisional codename, not a cleared launch brand.
- Do not invest in final logos, domains, store assets or paid promotion under the name yet.
- Before the first public release, conduct Indian and target-market trademark searches, Play Store/App Store listing searches, package-name checks, domain/social checks and legal review.
- Preserve a rename path by keeping product strings centralized and avoiding the brand in persisted file formats or public APIs.
